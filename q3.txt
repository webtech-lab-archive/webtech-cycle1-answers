and tagline, navigation links, a banner image, About Us section and a food menu categorized into Starters, Main Course, Desserts and
Beverages. Display the food items along with their prices using a table or card-based layout and provide contact information and a
footer. Use headings, paragraphs, images, lists, tables, hyperlinks, sections and articles. Apply background images, font properties,
borders, padding, margins, Flexbox or Grid, border-radius using CSS. [50 Marks]
