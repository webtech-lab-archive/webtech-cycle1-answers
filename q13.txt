name and logo, navigation menu, banking services, savings and current account information, loan services, interest-rate information in
a table, customer benefits, a login or registration section and contact information. Use headings, paragraphs, images, lists, tables,
forms, input elements, buttons, hyperlinks and sections. Apply CSS properties including Flexbox, table styling, form styling, borders,
padding, margins, background colors, button styling. [50 Marks]
