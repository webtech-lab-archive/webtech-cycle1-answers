Q12. Design and develop a static webpage for an Online Job Recruitment Portal using HTML and CSS. The webpage should contain
the portal name, navigation menu, welcome banner, job search section, job categories and at least three job listings displaying job title,
company name, location and salary. Each job listing should contain an Apply Now button. Include a Career Tips section and footer.
Use headings, paragraphs, forms, input fields, buttons, lists, links, sections and articles. Apply CSS Grid, Flexbox, form styling,
borders, box-shadow, border-radius. [50 Marks]
