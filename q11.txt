contain the website name, navigation menu, food categories, at least four food-item cards with images, names, descriptions and
prices, Add to Cart buttons, special offers and a customer review section. Use headings, paragraphs, images, lists, buttons, hyperlinks,
sections and articles. Apply CSS Grid, Flexbox, box-shadow, border-radius, padding, margin. [50 Marks]
